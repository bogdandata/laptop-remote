Connect this phone

  1. On the laptop, start Laptop Remote (double-click start.bat).
  2. In its window, type L and press Enter. A link appears and is copied.
  3. Send the link to yourself (e-mail, Viber, WhatsApp…) and open it on this phone.

A link works only once and only for 15 minutes. After that this phone stays connected, with no PIN.
